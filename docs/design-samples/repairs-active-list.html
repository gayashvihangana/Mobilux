Repairs page

Three ways to open on every active repair

"Active" means everything not yet collected or cancelled: online requests, received, diagnosing, waiting for parts, repairing and ready for pickup. All three samples share the same search, the same New repair button and a Board / List switch, so the current board can stay as a second view for anyone who likes it. Each is shown on a 1366px laptop and on a phone.

Sample 1 Recommended

One list, like an online shop's orders page

  • Every active repair in one table: device, problem, customer, step, days waiting, money and the next button. Nothing is squashed or cut off on a laptop.
  • Step chips with counts on top ("All active 20, Ready 6…"). Tap one to narrow the list; "All active" is the default.
  • Sorted by days waiting, oldest first, so late repairs sit at the top. Click any heading to sort by it.
  • Tick boxes for jobs on many at once (e.g. send a reminder to everyone ready for pickup).
  • This is what Shopify, Stripe and most repair-shop systems use. It's the easiest to scan with 20 or 200 repairs.

Sample 2

Grouped by what needs doing

  • Instead of the six steps, active repairs sit in three groups: Do now (late, ready to collect, part arrived), On the bench and Waiting on someone (customer or supplier).
  • Each card says why it's there ("Late by 104 days", "Customer hasn't replied in 3 days") and has the button for the next step.
  • Great for a busy counter: you never have to think about where to look. But exact steps are less visible, and very busy days make "Do now" long.
