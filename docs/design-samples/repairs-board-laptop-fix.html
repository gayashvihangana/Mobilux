The problem

On a laptop the five columns are about 190px wide

Half of every card is a big phone picture, so the device name wraps, the problem is cut after about 15 letters ("Keyboard keys not r…"), and "Remind / Picked up" are squashed side by side. Both options below are drawn at the real size of a 1366 × 768 laptop screen. Phones stay as they are now (one column, swipe between steps).

Option A Recommended

Compact cards: all five steps still on one screen

  • The big picture becomes a small device icon beside the name, so the text gets the whole card width.
  • The problem shows on two full lines instead of one cut-off line.
  • Name, repair number, customer and days waiting each get their own line; late ones in red.
  • Two buttons become one main button plus a small Remind icon, so nothing is squashed.
  • About three cards per column fit on the screen without scrolling (now it is under two).
  • Online requests become a slim strip, giving the board more height.
