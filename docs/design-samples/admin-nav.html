Notes. The sidebar here is shortened for the picture. The real one lists every section your account can open: Today, Repairs, Customers, Payments; Shop (Orders, Products, Loyalty, Reviews); Numbers (Shop numbers, Reports, Bench errors, Notes, Activity); Setup (Staff, Settings). Staff accounts only see what they are allowed to use, as now. Light and dark theme switch and sign out sit behind your name at the bottom of the sidebar, and behind the bell and avatar on a phone. The counts are open repairs and payments waiting to be checked.
