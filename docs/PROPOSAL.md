## The problem

Most repair shops run on a paper book and a WhatsApp thread.

That works until it doesn't:

- **"Is my phone ready?"** — the same call, ten times a day, pulling a technician
  off the bench every time.
- **A device goes missing in the system.** It was logged on a page that got torn,
  or it was never written down at all.
- **Nobody knows what a repair actually earned.** The parts cost is in one
  place, what the customer paid is in another, and the profit is a guess.
- **A customer disputes a price** and there is no record of what was quoted.
- **A returning customer gets no recognition** — the shop can't easily see they've
  been in four times this year.
- **No proof of a warranty claim** when the same fault comes back in three weeks.

None of this is a technology problem. It's a *record* problem. But it costs real
money — in lost repairs, in arguments, and in customers who go somewhere that
feels more organised.

## What Mobilux does

One system for the whole job: the device comes in, the customer watches it move,
the bill goes out, the money is recorded.

### For your customers

- **They track their own repair.** A link (or the QR code on their bill) shows
  exactly where their device is — received, diagnosed, waiting for parts, being
  repaired, ready. No account needed.
- **They stop calling to ask.** That alone is why most shops keep it.
- **A proper printed invoice**, itemised, with your shop's name and bank details.
- **They pay by bank transfer and upload the receipt** — you verify it in one
  click, and they get an email confirming it.
- **A loyalty discount that grows** with every repair they bring you.
- **They can buy accessories** from your storefront.

### For you and your team

- **One board of every open repair**, sorted by what needs attention today.
- **The full record of each device** — what came in with it, what was wrong, what
  parts went in, what they cost, who worked on it, what the customer paid.
- **What each repair actually earned**, calculated, not guessed.
- **Itemised bills** you build in about a minute, printed or sent as PDF.
- **Bench errors tracked honestly** — if something went wrong on your bench, the
  system helps you tell the customer, offer a free fix, and record their answer.
  It turns your worst conversations into a documented process.
- **Reports** — revenue, repair volume, what's costing you money.
- **Separate logins for your technicians**, so they can run the bench and the
  counter without seeing your revenue figures.
- **Customer reviews** you approve before they appear on your site.

## Common questions

**Is my customer data safe?**
Your shop gets its own private database — no other shop can see it. Passwords are
encrypted. The system is backed up, and I can restore it to any point in the last
[[N]] days.

**What happens if the website goes down?**
It runs on the same hosting used by large companies, so this is rare. With a
monthly plan, fixing it is my job. Your data is backed up either way.

**Do my customers need to create an account?**
No. Walk-in customers get a tracking link and never sign up. Customers who want
their full repair history and loyalty discounts can create one.

**Can I change my shop details later?**
Yes — name, phone, address, opening hours, bank accounts, all from the settings
page. No need to contact me.

**What if I stop paying the monthly fee?**
The system keeps working. You lose support, updates and my time — not the
software or your data.

**Can my technicians use it without seeing my revenue?**
Yes. Staff accounts can run repairs and issue bills but cannot see reports,
profit figures, or your bank settings.

**Do I need to buy computers?**
No. It works in a web browser on whatever you already have, including phones.

**Can I get out of it?**
It's your data. Ask and I'll export everything for you.
